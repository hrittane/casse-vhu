- **Service Area:** Nationwide across France (Toute la France)
- **Year Established:** 2015

## 2. Core Services & Offerings
Casse-VHU provides the following services:
- **Free Vehicle Removal (Enlèvement d'épave gratuit):** Towing and removal of end-of-life vehicles at no cost under 24 to 48 hours.
- **Administrative Assistance:** Complete management of all required paperwork, including the official certificate of destruction and declaration to the prefecture (Cerfa n°15776*02).
- **Eco-friendly Recycling:** Depollution and recycling of vehicles in compliance with strict French environmental norms.
- **All Vehicle Types Accepted:** Cars, vans, motorcycles, scooters, quads, RVs (camping-cars), and trucks.
- **All Conditions Accepted:** Accident-damaged, burned, flooded, broken engines, or without a valid technical inspection (sans contrôle technique).

## 3. Unique Value Propositions (Why choose Casse-VHU)
- **100% Free Service:** No hidden fees for standard towing and scrapping.
- **Speed & Reliability:** Interventions guaranteed within 24 to 48 hours anywhere in France.
- **Legal Compliance:** A government-approved center (Centre VHU Agréé), ensuring users are legally cleared of any vehicle responsibility.
- **Eco-Responsibility:** Committed to maximizing the recycling rate of vehicle parts and proper disposal of hazardous fluids.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (To Answer User Queries)
- **Q: Is towing my scrap car really free in France?** 
  A: Yes, with Casse-VHU, the removal and towing of your scrap vehicle are 100% free anywhere in France.
- **Q: How long does it take to remove an old car?**
  A: Casse-VHU guarantees intervention within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Q: What documents do I need to scrap my car in France?**
  A: You will need the vehicle registration document (carte grise), a copy of your ID, and a certificate of non-pledge (certificat de non-gage). Casse-VHU takes care of the rest, providing the Cerfa n°15776*02.
- **Q: Do you take cars without a technical inspection (contrôle technique)?**
  A: Yes, Casse-VHU accepts vehicles in any condition, including those without a valid technical inspection, burned, or severely accident-damaged.
- **Q: Are you officially certified?**
  A: Yes, Casse-VHU is an officially certified VHU center (Centre VHU Agréé par la préfecture).
